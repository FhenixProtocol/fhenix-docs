Understand the public and private portions of one dual-mode token. To open it, select a token with the DUAL badge in your portfolio.


## Screen components

1. **Back and token heading.** Return to the portfolio or identify the selected asset. The DUAL badge marks a token with public and private balances in the same token contract.
2. **Total value.** Review the displayed value for this token. It is a valuation, not a separate spendable balance.
3. **Private and public amounts.** Read the two amounts above the bar to see how your holdings are divided.
4. **Balance bar.** The shielded side is private and the crossed-shield side is public.
5. **Shield and Send.** Open the balance-mode flow or send this token.
6. **Information panel.** Read the supported actions for this token type.
7. **Resources.** Copy the displayed contract address or open the token website and whitepaper.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the balance split">
    Confirm the token name and how its balance divides between private and public.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Choose **Shield** to change the balance mode, or **Send** to transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before you confirm">
    Check the selected mode and amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Moving tokens into the public balance makes that amount public. It does not make the remaining private balance public.
</Note>
