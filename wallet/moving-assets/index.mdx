---
title: "Moving assets"
description: "Shield, unshield, claim, send, and receive confidential tokens, and check the result in Activity."
---

These screens move value between public and private form, and between wallets. Fees in the screenshots are examples, not fixed charges.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Shield tokens" href="/wallet/moving-assets/shield-tokens">
    Move an amount from a public balance into confidential form.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unshield tokens" href="/wallet/moving-assets/unshield-tokens">
    Move a private amount back into public form through Unshield, In progress, and Claim.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Claim unshielded tokens" href="/wallet/moving-assets/claim-unshielded-tokens">
    Complete an unshielding request once its tokens are ready to claim.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send tokens" href="/wallet/moving-assets/send-tokens">
    Transfer a supported token using either its private or public balance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Receive tokens" href="/wallet/moving-assets/receive-tokens">
    Share your receiving address or prepare a request for a specific token amount.
  </Card>
  <Card title="View activity" href="/wallet/moving-assets/view-activity">
    Review actions recorded by the wallet and inspect their transaction references.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
