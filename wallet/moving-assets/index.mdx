These screens move value between public and private form, and between wallets. Fees in the screenshots are examples, not fixed charges.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Shield tokens" icon="shield" href="/wallet/moving-assets/shield-tokens">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Unshield tokens" icon="lock-open" href="/wallet/moving-assets/unshield-tokens">
    Move a private amount back into public form through Unshield, In progress, and Claim.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Claim unshielded tokens" icon="hand-holding-dollar" href="/wallet/moving-assets/claim-unshielded-tokens">
    Complete an unshielding request once its tokens are ready to claim.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send tokens" icon="paper-plane" href="/wallet/moving-assets/send-tokens">
    Transfer a supported token using either its private or public balance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="View activity" icon="clock-rotate-left" href="/wallet/moving-assets/view-activity">
    Review actions recorded by the wallet and inspect their transaction references.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
