Complete an unshielding request once its tokens are ready to claim. To open it, open the token details and look for **Claim now** and the claimable amount.


## Screen components

1. **Balance split.** Review the private, public, and pending portions of the token.
2. **Shielded and public rows.** Distinguish the confidential balance from the public balance.
3. **Claimable amount.** Shows the amount awaiting the final claim action.
4. **Claim now.** Starts the final step for the available amount.
5. **Other actions.** Shield and Send remain separate actions. Starting another action does not replace Claim.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait until the request is ready">
    **Claim now** appears when the amount is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the claim">
    Confirm the claimable asset and amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Select **Claim now** and complete any wallet confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Wait for completion, then check the public balance and activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The dual-token detail screen also has a **Claim now** action. A pending amount is not a completed claim.
</Note>
