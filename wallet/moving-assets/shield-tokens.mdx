Move an amount from a public balance into confidential form. To open it, choose **Shield** from the portfolio or token details.


## Screen components

1. **Shield and Unshield tabs.** Select Shield for public-to-private conversion.
2. **You deposit.** Enter the amount and choose the token. The balance label helps you check available funds.
3. **Amount shortcuts.** 25%, 50%, 75%, and max fill the amount using the available balance.
4. **You Receive.** Review the confidential asset and amount you receive.
5. **Privacy notice.** Explains that the resulting balance is encrypted and viewable with the appropriate permission.
6. **Progress tracker.** Follow the required steps. The wrapped-token flow shown here has Approve, Shield, and Done.
7. **Shield button and fee.** Start or continue the flow. Review the wallet request and the current fee estimate.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the shield details">
    Select **Shield**, a token, and an amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result and fee">
    Check **You Receive** and the fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve if requested">
    If the wallet requests an approval, confirm the token approval in your connected wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shield and wait">
    Continue with **Shield** and wait for completion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Approval authorizes token use. It does not shield tokens. Follow every required step.
</Note>
