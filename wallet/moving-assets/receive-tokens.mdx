Share your receiving address or prepare a request for a specific token amount. To open it, open the transfer screen and select **Receive**.


## Screen components

1. **Receive tab.** Switches from sending funds to receiving them.
2. **Custom Amount.** Enables the amount-specific request controls.
3. **Amount to ask and token.** Set the requested amount and asset when Custom Amount is enabled.
4. **QR area.** Displays the generated receive code. Share or scan the code produced in your session.
5. **Wallet address and copy.** Copy your full receiving address for the sender.
6. **Generate QR.** Create the receive code for the selected request.
7. **Tap to Pay.** An additional receive option shown in the design. If it is available in your environment, follow its on-screen instructions.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the address">
    Confirm the connected address is the intended recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Copy the address, or set an optional custom amount and generate the QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the sender what to use">
    Give the sender the network and token to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your balance">
    Check your balance after the transfer settles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A payment request is not a completed transfer. The QR area and address in the screenshot are examples. Do not send funds to them.
</Warning>
