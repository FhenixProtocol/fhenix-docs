Transfer a supported token using either its private or public balance. To open it, choose **Send** from the portfolio or token details.


## Screen components

1. **Send and Receive tabs.** Keep Send selected to make a transfer.
2. **Privacy mode.** The shield controls and the Private transaction label show the selected transfer mode. Check it before you continue.
3. **Recipient address.** Paste the destination wallet address and verify it.
4. **Amount and token.** Choose the asset and amount. The balance shown corresponds to the selected mode.
5. **Amount shortcuts.** Use a percentage or max to fill the amount, then recheck it.
6. **Recipient receives.** Review the destination asset and amount.
7. **Send and fee.** Continue to your connected wallet and review its transaction request.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the mode">
    Choose private or public transfer mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the transfer details">
    Enter the recipient, token, and amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Check **Recipient receives** and the fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Select **Send**, confirm in your wallet, and monitor completion in Activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Private transfers protect the amount. They do not make wallet addresses anonymous. Public transfers expose their amounts onchain.
</Note>
