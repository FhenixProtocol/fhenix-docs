---
title: "Moving assets"
description: "Shield, unshield, claim, send, and receive confidential tokens, and check the result in Activity."
---

These screens move value between public and private form, and between wallets. Fees in the screenshots are examples, not fixed charges.

## Shield tokens

Move an amount from a public balance into confidential form. To open it, choose **Shield** from the portfolio or token details.

<Frame>
<img src="/wallet/images/shield-tokens.png" alt="Shield form with a deposit amount, a You Receive summary, a progress tracker showing Approve, Shield, and Done, and a fee estimate" />
</Frame>

### Screen components

1. **Shield and Unshield tabs.** Select Shield for public-to-private conversion.
2. **You deposit.** Enter the amount and choose the token. The balance label helps you check available funds.
3. **Amount shortcuts.** 25%, 50%, 75%, and max fill the amount using the available balance.
4. **You Receive.** Review the confidential asset and amount you receive.
5. **Privacy notice.** Explains that the resulting balance is encrypted and viewable with the appropriate permission.
6. **Progress tracker.** Follow the required steps. The wrapped-token flow shown here has Approve, Shield, and Done.
7. **Shield button and fee.** Start or continue the flow. Review the wallet request and the current fee estimate.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the shield details">
    Select **Shield**, a token, and an amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result and fee">
    Check **You Receive** and the fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve if requested">
    If the wallet requests an approval, confirm the token approval in your connected wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shield and wait">
    Continue with **Shield** and wait for completion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Approval authorizes token use. It does not shield tokens. Follow every required step.
</Note>

## Unshield tokens

Move a private amount back into public form. Unshielding has three stages: Unshield, In progress, and Claim. To open it, open **Shield / Unshield** and select **Unshield**.

<Frame>
<img src="/wallet/images/unshield-tokens.png" alt="Unshield form with an amount, a public You Receive summary, and a three-stage tracker showing Unshield, In progress, and Claim" />
</Frame>

### Screen components

1. **Unshield tab.** Check that Unshield is selected before you enter an amount.
2. **You unshield.** Choose the confidential asset and enter an amount within its available private balance.
3. **Amount shortcuts.** Use 25%, 50%, 75%, or max to fill the amount.
4. **You Receive.** Check the public asset and amount shown by the live application.
5. **Privacy notice.** Unshielding exposes the selected amount as a public balance. This action is visible onchain.
6. **Three-stage tracker.** Unshield starts the request. In progress means processing is underway. Claim is the final action.
7. **Unshield button and fee.** Submit the request through your connected wallet after you review its details.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the unshield details">
    Select the token and amount, and review the public output.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in your wallet">
    Confirm **Unshield** in your connected wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait through In progress">
    Processing continues until the amount is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    When Claim is available, complete it to receive the public balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A confirmed Unshield request is not the end of this flow. Complete Claim before you treat the amount as an available public balance.
</Warning>

## Claim unshielded tokens

Complete an unshielding request once its tokens are ready to claim. To open it, open the token details and look for **Claim now** and the claimable amount.

<Frame>
<img src="/wallet/images/claim-unshielded-tokens.png" alt="Token details with a balance split, a claimable amount, and a Claim now button" />
</Frame>

### Screen components

1. **Balance split.** Review the private, public, and pending portions of the token.
2. **Shielded and public rows.** Distinguish the confidential balance from the public balance.
3. **Claimable amount.** Shows the amount awaiting the final claim action.
4. **Claim now.** Starts the final step for the available amount.
5. **Other actions.** Shield and Send remain separate actions. Starting another action does not replace Claim.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait until the request is ready">
    **Claim now** appears when the amount is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the claim">
    Confirm the claimable asset and amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Select **Claim now** and complete any wallet confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Wait for completion, then check the public balance and activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The dual-token detail screen also has a **Claim now** action. A pending amount is not a completed claim.
</Note>

## Send tokens

Transfer a supported token using either its private or public balance. To open it, choose **Send** from the portfolio or token details.

<Frame>
<img src="/wallet/images/send-tokens.png" alt="Send form with a privacy mode control, recipient address, amount and token fields, a Recipient receives summary, and a fee" />
</Frame>

### Screen components

1. **Send and Receive tabs.** Keep Send selected to make a transfer.
2. **Privacy mode.** The shield controls and the Private transaction label show the selected transfer mode. Check it before you continue.
3. **Recipient address.** Paste the destination wallet address and verify it.
4. **Amount and token.** Choose the asset and amount. The balance shown corresponds to the selected mode.
5. **Amount shortcuts.** Use a percentage or max to fill the amount, then recheck it.
6. **Recipient receives.** Review the destination asset and amount.
7. **Send and fee.** Continue to your connected wallet and review its transaction request.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the mode">
    Choose private or public transfer mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the transfer details">
    Enter the recipient, token, and amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Check **Recipient receives** and the fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Select **Send**, confirm in your wallet, and monitor completion in Activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Private transfers protect the amount. They do not make wallet addresses anonymous. Public transfers expose their amounts onchain.
</Note>

## Receive tokens

Share your receiving address or prepare a request for a specific token amount. To open it, open the transfer screen and select **Receive**.

<Frame>
<img src="/wallet/images/receive-tokens.png" alt="Receive screen with a Custom Amount toggle, a QR code area, the wallet address with a copy control, and a Generate QR button" />
</Frame>

### Screen components

1. **Receive tab.** Switches from sending funds to receiving them.
2. **Custom Amount.** Enables the amount-specific request controls.
3. **Amount to ask and token.** Set the requested amount and asset when Custom Amount is enabled.
4. **QR area.** Displays the generated receive code. Share or scan the code produced in your session.
5. **Wallet address and copy.** Copy your full receiving address for the sender.
6. **Generate QR.** Create the receive code for the selected request.
7. **Tap to Pay.** An additional receive option shown in the design. If it is available in your environment, follow its on-screen instructions.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the address">
    Confirm the connected address is the intended recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Copy the address, or set an optional custom amount and generate the QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the sender what to use">
    Give the sender the network and token to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your balance">
    Check your balance after the transfer settles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A payment request is not a completed transfer. The QR area and address in the screenshot are examples. Do not send funds to them.
</Warning>

## View activity

Review actions recorded by the wallet and inspect their transaction references. To open it, select **Activity** next to **Tokens** in the portfolio.

<Frame>
<img src="/wallet/images/view-activity.png" alt="Activity list with action labels, statuses, times, token amounts, and transaction reference links" />
</Frame>

### Screen components

1. **Tokens and Activity tabs.** Switch between balances and recorded actions.
2. **Action label.** Identifies the kind of action on each row.
3. **Status.** Shows the reported state of that action.
4. **Time.** Shows when the action occurred or how long ago it was recorded.
5. **Token amount and value.** Shows the amount and any available value for that entry.
6. **Transaction reference.** Use the copy control or external link to inspect the transaction in the network explorer.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the action">
    Open Activity and locate the relevant action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read its state">
    Check its status and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the transaction">
    Use its transaction reference for further inspection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish an unshield">
    For an unshielding request, return to token details and complete Claim when it is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A missing entry does not prove that a transaction did not occur. Check the connected wallet and the transaction reference.
</Note>
