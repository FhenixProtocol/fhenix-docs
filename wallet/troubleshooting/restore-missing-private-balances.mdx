Restore your private-balance view when a permit is missing or unusable. To open it, follow the **Permit missing** banner or an inline **Renew permit to decrypt** message.


## Screen components

1. **Masked total.** The private portion appears as a placeholder while any available public value stays visible.
2. **Permit missing banner.** Explains that a permit is needed to display private balances.
3. **Privacy Center button.** Opens the place to create or renew the permit.
4. **Token rows.** Masked amounts and renewal labels identify balances that cannot currently be displayed.
5. **Dismiss control.** Closes the banner. It does not create or renew a permit.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the account">
    Confirm your connected account and network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Privacy Center">
    Select **Privacy Center**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or renew a permit">
    Create a permit if none is available, or renew the expired permit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign and return">
    Complete the message signature and return to the portfolio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A masked amount is not zero. It means the wallet cannot currently show that private amount. Do not resend a transfer only because its balance is hidden.
</Warning>
