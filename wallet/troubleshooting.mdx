title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Use the Privacy Center to fix missing permits, expired access, timed-out balance reads, and decryption failures."
---

Most wallet problems come from a missing or expired viewing permit, a timed-out balance request, or too little gas. Start in the Privacy Center, which lists each error and its fix.

## Use the Privacy Center

Check viewing permissions, token allowances, and errors in one place. To open it, use the wallet privacy controls or an error banner.

<Frame>
<img src="/wallet/images/privacy-center.png" alt="Privacy Center with ACP status, an Errors and Bugs list, a Permits section with Renew, and an Allowance section" />
</Frame>

### Screen components

1. **ACP status.** Shows the reported access-permit status. Check the individual error rows as well as the summary.
2. **Errors and Bugs.** Each row names the affected balance or action and its error, such as ACP Expired, Time out, or Not enough gas.
3. **Retry controls.** A retry icon appears for retryable failures such as a timed-out balance request.
4. **Permits.** Review active and expired permits. Use **Renew** when your viewing authorization needs renewal.
5. **Allowance.** Review token-use permissions separately from viewing permits. Rows show an asset, address, public/private label, limit, and any expiration.
6. **Allowance actions.** Use the available action to review removal or renewal. Read its confirmation and any connected-wallet request.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the affected item">
    Find the affected token or action in the error list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the fix">
    Renew a missing or expired viewing permit, retry a timed-out query, or add gas for a gas error.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recheck">
    Return to the wallet and check the affected balance or action again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Permits control access to encrypted information. Allowances authorize token use by an address or contract. Renewing one does not necessarily repair the other.
</Note>

## Restore missing private balances

Restore your private-balance view when a permit is missing or unusable. To open it, follow the **Permit missing** banner or an inline **Renew permit to decrypt** message.

<Frame>
<img src="/wallet/images/permit-missing.png" alt="Portfolio with a masked private total, a Permit missing banner, and masked token amounts with renewal labels" />
</Frame>

### Screen components

1. **Masked total.** The private portion appears as a placeholder while any available public value stays visible.
2. **Permit missing banner.** Explains that a permit is needed to display private balances.
3. **Privacy Center button.** Opens the place to create or renew the permit.
4. **Token rows.** Masked amounts and renewal labels identify balances that cannot currently be displayed.
5. **Dismiss control.** Closes the banner. It does not create or renew a permit.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the account">
    Confirm your connected account and network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Privacy Center">
    Select **Privacy Center**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or renew a permit">
    Create a permit if none is available, or renew the expired permit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign and return">
    Complete the message signature and return to the portfolio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A masked amount is not zero. It means the wallet cannot currently show that private amount. Do not resend a transfer only because its balance is hidden.
</Warning>

## Resolve decryption failures

Find and address a failed private-balance read. To open it, select **Privacy Center** in the Decryption Failed banner.

<Frame>
<img src="/wallet/images/decryption-failed.png" alt="Portfolio with a masked private value, a Decryption Failed banner, and an inline error on one token" />
</Frame>

### Screen components

1. **Masked private value.** Shows that part of the balance could not be displayed.
2. **Decryption Failed banner.** Distinguishes a failed read from a successful balance display.
3. **Privacy Center action.** Opens details that help identify the affected request and error.
4. **Inline token error.** Marks the individual token balance that failed.
5. **Close control.** Dismisses the notice without resolving the underlying failure.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the error">
    Open the Privacy Center and read the specific error.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the permit and account">
    Check the active permit and the connected account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Renew or retry">
    Renew the permit if required, or use the retry control for a timed-out request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recheck">
    Recheck the balance. If the error persists, record the token, network, error, and time for support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A failed read does not establish that funds were lost or a transfer failed. Check transaction status separately before you repeat a send.
</Note>
