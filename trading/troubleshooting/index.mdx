Most issues come from a missing permit, a reveal that has not been completed, or the all-or-nothing rule for confidential transfers. A **Needs you** badge on the **My Orders** or **Activity** tab marks anything waiting for you.

## Your new order is not in the book

The order was created and escrowed but not listed. An order stays pending, and is not visible or fillable, until the **List order** step reveals its tier.

Open **My Orders**, find the order, and complete the listing step. Listing carries no bond.

## A fill went through but nothing moved

Confidential transfers are all or nothing. If your balance could not cover the full payment, nothing was paid and nothing was released.

Check your decrypted balance, lower the amount, and fill again.

## The order does not cover your whole amount

The book row does not show **Covers full amount**, and the **Fill outcome** in the **Fill order** dialog is not **Fills in full**. The order's remaining size is smaller than your amount.

Fill it anyway and pay only for the part that fills, pick another order as well, or lower your amount.

## You do not have enough ETH

Every transaction needs ETH for gas, and a fill also posts the refundable bond shown in the **Fill order** dialog.

Add ETH to your wallet on the test network, then retry. Makers need gas only; listing and top-ups carry no bond.

## An extra Allow or Set operator step appears

The first time you trade a token, the OTC contract needs your permission to move it. Fills show this as **Allow**. Creating and topping up orders show it as **Set operator**.

Confirm the step in your wallet, then continue with the next step in the tracker.

## Only your own orders are on this pair

**Best matches** is empty and says only your own orders are open on the pair.

Pick another pair, using the order counts in the [token selector](/trading/trading-screens/choose-a-token), or post your own order and wait for a taker.

## Your order shows Delisted or Expired

- **Delisted:** the order was filled to nothing. Select **Top up** to relist it, or **Close order** to finish.
- **Expired:** the order passed its expiry and can no longer be filled. Select **Close order** to return the escrow to your wallet.

## Trading is paused or a token is delisted

You can still close orders, list pending orders, and complete reveals, so escrow and bonds can always be recovered. New orders, fills, and top-ups resume when trading reopens.

## You are on the wrong account or network

Check the connected address and network in the wallet header. Your orders, fills, and permits belong to the connected account.
