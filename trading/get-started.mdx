This page gets you ready to post or fill your first order. You need a wallet that holds ETH for gas and a confidential balance of the token you want to sell.

## Connect your wallet

Open [genesis.fhenix.io/trading](https://genesis.fhenix.io/trading), select **Connect**, and connect the account you want to trade with. Check the account and the network before you continue.

## Authorize private viewing

Create or confirm an Access Control Permit (ACP) when the app asks. The permit lets the app decrypt your own balances and order amounts for display. It does not move tokens. See [Create a viewing permit](/wallet/settings-and-permits/create-viewing-permit).

## Have the right assets

You need two things in your wallet:

- A confidential balance of the token you want to sell.
- Public ETH on the network for gas. Takers also need ETH for the refundable fill bond.

If your tokens are public, [shield them](/wallet/moving-assets/shield-tokens) in the wallet module first.
