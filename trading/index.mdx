The Fhenix Trading Module is a peer-to-peer venue for trading confidential tokens. A maker posts an order at a public price with an encrypted amount, and a taker fills it in a single atomic transaction. Read this page to understand what the book shows, what stays private, and why each action takes two transactions.

<Note>
Screenshots in this guide contain example values. Use the tokens, prices, and amounts shown in your own session.
</Note>

## What is public and what is private

Prices are public. Order sizes and fill amounts stay encrypted end to end.

| Public | Private (encrypted) |
|---|---|
| Maker address, both tokens, price, expiry, size tier, and listing state | Order amounts: escrowed, remaining, deposited over time, and every fill amount |

The maker can decrypt their own remaining and deposited amounts to follow fill progress. Nobody else can, including the venue.

## What a size tier is

The book never shows an order's amount. It shows a size tier: a dollar range computed under FHE (fully homomorphic encryption) from the encrypted amount.

| Tier | Range |
|---|---|
| S | $100 to $1K |
| M | $1K to $10K |
| L | $10K to $100K |
| XL | $100K to $1M |
| XXL | Above $1M |

The tier is valued at the venue's reference price for the token and is used only for display. Fills always settle at the maker's price.

## Why every action takes two transactions

Tokens move in the first transaction. The new remaining amount is encrypted, so a threshold of network nodes decrypts its tier, and a second transaction publishes it. This second transaction is the reveal.

The reveal never moves tokens; it only updates what the book shows. Listing, relisting, and delisting happen only on a reveal, so the book never shows a size the chain has not confirmed.

## What the fill bond is

Filling an order posts a small, refundable ETH bond, about $5, so the reveal after the fill is not forgotten. Reveal within the exclusive window and the whole bond comes back.

After the window, anyone may reveal on your behalf and keep a bounty from the bond. The rest still returns to you. Makers' reveals, for listing and top-ups, carry no bond.

## How an order moves through its lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Post an order">
    Choose the token you sell, the token you want back, a price, and an optional expiry. The amount is encrypted in your browser and escrowed. The order is pending: escrowed, but not yet visible or fillable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List it with a reveal">
    A second transaction, with no bond, proves the order's size tier and lists it in the book.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A taker fills it">
    The taker pays with the token you asked for and receives yours from escrow, atomically. The fill is capped at the order's encrypted remaining amount and at what the taker can pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The new tier is revealed">
    Every fill and top-up records a new encrypted tier, and the reveal updates the book. An empty tier delists the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deplete, revive, or close">
    An order filled to nothing is delisted but keeps its escrow slot; a top-up plus a reveal relists it. Closing is final and returns the remaining escrow to the maker.
  </Step>
</Steps>
